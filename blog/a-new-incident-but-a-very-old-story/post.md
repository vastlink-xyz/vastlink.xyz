A new incident, but a very old story: the crypto industry just witnessed what’s being called the largest bank heist in human history, with billions drained in the recent Binance hack.

At **Vastlink**, this is a sobering reminder of a point we’ve been making for a while now—digital asset security isn't just a technical challenge you can solve with code alone.

Two core principles we keep coming back to:

**1. Operations are critical.**

Segregation of duties, clear approval workflows, and strict change management are the bedrock of real custody. At the end of the day, your security is only as strong as the day-to-day processes backing it up.

**2. Over-concentration is a magnet for trouble.**

Holding massive pools of funds in single, centralized targets creates irresistible honeypots for attackers. Spreading out risk and strictly capping exposure are basic, non-negotiable risk management habits.

The takeaway couldn't be clearer: great cryptography doesn't automatically mean a secure custody setup. Even as the industry shifts away from traditional private keys toward keyless solutions like MPC and TSS, operational discipline hasn't become obsolete—it’s actually become far more critical.

If the industry is going to mature, we need sharper operational governance, smarter risk management, and far better infrastructure design across the board.
